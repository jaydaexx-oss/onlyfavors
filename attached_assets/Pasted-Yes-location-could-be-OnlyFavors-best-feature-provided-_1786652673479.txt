Yes—location could be OnlyFavors’ best feature, provided it is designed as a safety tool rather than a people-tracking map.
Use a location ladder:
Before booking: Show only city, neighborhood and approximate distance.
After acceptance: Reveal the agreed public venue—not either person’s address.
During the favor: Enable temporary check-ins and optional live sharing.
After checkout: Stop sharing automatically and delete precise location after a short, disclosed retention period.
During an emergency: Share exact location with the user’s trusted contact or emergency services.
Strong location features could include:
Near Me: Find available companions within a chosen radius
SafeSpot Map: Recommended cafés, hotels and public venues
Meet Here: Both users approve the meeting point
Geofenced Check-In: Confirm arrival without sharing the route taken
Trust Circle Link: Temporary booking map for selected contacts
Missed Check-In Alert: Notify trusted contacts if someone does not respond
Walk Me There: Continue safety sharing until the person reaches transportation
Expiring Location Links: Automatically become unusable after the booking
Important restrictions:
Never display companions as live pins
Never expose home or work addresses
Never track people outside active bookings
Never sell or use precise location for advertising
Require clear consent from both sides
Let either person stop ordinary sharing immediately
Use an obvious indicator whenever location is active
The signature interface could be a mulberry map with mint SafeSpots, showing:
Three verified companions available near Waikīkī this evening

That feels useful and safe without revealing anyone’s precise whereabouts.

After location, I’d add features that improve matching, reduce awkwardness and create repeat bookings.
Matching
Vibe Check: Match by conversational energy—quiet, playful, outgoing, supportive or adventurous.
Comfort Dial: Select preferred talking level, physical distance, photography, alcohol and group interaction.
Voice Preview: Short audio introductions instead of relying entirely on photos.
Favor Builder: Describe the occasion and receive three suitable companions, venues and estimated totals.
Mutual Acceptance: Both people review the request before anyone can pay or message.
Better experiences
Shared Plan: Venue, schedule, tickets, attire and meeting instructions in one place.
Conversation Deck: Optional prompts and games for avoiding awkward silences.
Time Extension: Both users approve and pay for another hour inside the app.
Standby Match: Quickly find a replacement if someone cancels.
Duo Booking: Book two companions for concerts, travel or group events.
Accessibility Match: Filter by language, mobility awareness, sensory preferences and communication style.
Retention
Trusted Circle: Privately save companions the customer feels comfortable rebooking.
Favor Again: Repeat a previous booking in two taps.
Recurring Favors: Weekly walks, study sessions or monthly events.
Private Memory Card: A shared photo, venue and note—saved only with mutual consent.
Gift a Favor: Purchase an activity-specific credit for someone else.
Loyalty Credits: Reward completed bookings instead of profile popularity.
Companion quality
Specialty Badges: Local guide, event etiquette, language practice, photography, gaming or accessibility-aware.
Availability Calendar: Hourly, half-day and full-day pricing.
Companion Academy: Short training in boundaries, safety, communication and inclusive behavior.
Reliability Protection: Reward attendance and responsiveness—not attractiveness or booking volume.
Anonymous Customer Risk Feedback: Safety information available only to moderators and relevant companions.
One standout feature
Build Favor Mode, a live booking screen that activates when the meetup begins:
Meeting-point navigation
Temporary location sharing
Check-in timer
Boundaries summary
Trusted-contact status
Extend or end booking
Discreet assistance button
Mutual checkout and payment release
That would turn OnlyFavors from a profile marketplace into a complete, safety-first companionship experience.
