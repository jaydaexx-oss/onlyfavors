The strongest additions should reinforce privacy, safety and genuine human connection. Avoid turning it into another overloaded dating marketplace.
Signature features
SafeSpot Network
Partner with cafés, hotels and public venues. Approved locations display an OnlyFavors shield and provide:
QR arrival check-in
Staff awareness
Clearly marked meeting points
Emergency assistance
Venue discounts
This could become a distinctive, visible part of the brand.
Boundary Receipt
Before booking, both people approve:
Activity and location
Start and end time
Transportation expectations
Physical-contact boundaries
Photography permission
Alcohol expectations
Topics or behaviors to avoid
Changes require both people to approve again.
Trust Circle
Users select trusted contacts who can receive:
Booking details
Companion’s verified identity
Live check-in status
Missed-check-in alerts
Emergency location sharing
Exact location should be collected only during the booking and deleted afterward.
Protected ChatChat unlocks only after an accepted request and payment authorization
Phone numbers and emails remain hidden
Disappearing media
Optional watermarks on profile photos
Easy blocking and reporting
Reported conversations can be reviewed by the safety team

Don’t promise end-to-end encryption if moderation staff need to investigate reported messages.
Discreet Exit
A user can press “Help me leave” during a booking. The app can:
Send a believable cancellation notification
Alert a trusted contact
Provide directions to the nearest SafeSpot
Contact emergency services when explicitly requested
End location sharing after confirming safety
Features that improve retention
Favorite Circle: Privately save and rebook trusted companions
Favor Again: Repeat a successful experience in two taps
Voice Introductions: Short personality-focused audio prompts
Shared Itinerary: Venue, tickets, dress code and meeting instructions
Duo Favors: Book two companions or attend as a small group
Gift a Favor: Purchase a safe, activity-specific gift card
Availability Now: Show companions available within the next few hours
Reliability Score: Based on attendance and communication—not popularity or appearance
Private Feedback: “Did you feel respected and safe?” never shown publicly
Accessibility Preferences: Mobility, sensory, communication and venue requirements
Companion protections
Companions need equal protection:
Customers verified before requesting
Complete control over activities and boundaries
Decline requests without ranking penalties
Block particular dates, areas or users
Cancellation compensation
Harassment detection and rapid reporting
Optional public alias
Approximate location only
Automatic payout after safe checkout
Private customer-risk history reviewed by moderators
Additional revenue
Later, OnlyFavors can earn from:
$99/year optional Plus membership
Venue partnerships
Event-ticket and restaurant referrals
Gift cards
Corporate event companion packages
Concierge matching
Cancellation protection
Never sell profile placement as “best match,” and never charge for essential privacy or emergency features.
If we prioritize only three signature additions, I’d choose SafeSpot Network, Boundary Receipt and Trust Circle. Together they give OnlyFavors a safety system competitors cannot copy with a simple UI update.

Yes—location could be OnlyFavors’ best feature, provided it is designed as a safety tool rather than a people-tracking map.
Use a location ladder:
Before booking: Show only city, neighborhood and approximate distance.
After acceptance: Reveal the agreed public venue—not either person’s address.
During the favor: Enable temporary check-ins and optional live sharing.
After checkout: Stop sharing automatically and delete precise location after a short, disclosed retention period.
During an emergency: Share exact location with the user’s trusted contact or emergency services.
Strong location features could include:
Near Me: Find available companions within a chosen radius
SafeSpot Map: Recommended cafés, hotels and public venues
Meet Here: Both users approve the meeting point
Geofenced Check-In: Confirm arrival without sharing the route taken
Trust Circle Link: Temporary booking map for selected contacts
Missed Check-In Alert: Notify trusted contacts if someone does not respond
Walk Me There: Continue safety sharing until the person reaches transportation
Expiring Location Links: Automatically become unusable after the booking
Important restrictions:
Never display companions as live pins
Never expose home or work addresses
Never track people outside active bookings
Never sell or use precise location for advertising
Require clear consent from both sides
Let either person stop ordinary sharing immediately
Use an obvious indicator whenever location is active
The signature interface could be a mulberry map with mint SafeSpots, showing:
Three verified companions available near Waikīkī this evening

That feels useful and safe without revealing anyone’s precise whereabouts.
